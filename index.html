Project Phases: What to Expect

  1. Consultation & Preparation

    October to December 2026

    • Faculty and student demonstrations
    • Focus groups
    • Testing of tools, integrations, and workflows
    • Mapping of Moodle content and functionality to Brightspace

  2. Decision

    December 2026

  3. Training & Readiness

    January to August 2027

    • Instructional staff and faculty workshops
    • Creation of onboarding and help guides
    • Provision of instructional sandboxes
    • Course design and readiness support

  4. Course Migration

    March to December 2027

    • Migration of existing Moodle courses to the new LMS

    If we stay with Moodle

    • Spring 2027 courses March to April
    • 2027–2028 courses May to July

    If we move to D2L Brightspace

    • Fall 2027 pilot courses March to June
    • 2027–2028 courses May to December

More detail about migration and launch will be shared once the Moodle vs. D2L decision is made in December 2026.
